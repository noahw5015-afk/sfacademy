Courses we provide

Two levels of first aid certification

Both delivered on-site by a Lifesaving Society certified instructor and fully compliant with Ontario workplace requirements.

Basic First Aid & CPR-C

8 hours

A focused, one-day certification covering the core skills every workplace should have on hand, including CPR-C, AED use and response to the most common emergencies. Ideal for lower-risk offices or teams needing essential coverage.

Entry level

Intermediate First Aid & CPR-C

16 hours

Our comprehensive certification and the level most Ontario employers need to stay compliant. A thorough, hands-on program that prepares staff to respond confidently to a wide range of workplace emergencies.

Most requested
Minimum 5 participants per session, with no maximum. Whether you're certifying a small team or several hundred staff across multiple floors, we'll scale the delivery to fit.

What your team will learn

Our courses prepare staff to respond confidently to a real emergency in the workplace. The Intermediate course covers everything below in depth; the Basic course focuses on the core life-saving essentials.

  • CPR-C & AED for adults, children, and infants
  • Responding to choking and airway emergencies
  • Bleeding control, wound care, and shock management
  • Recognizing heart attack, stroke, and medical emergencies
  • Head, neck, and spinal injury response
  • Burns, fractures, and sudden medical conditions
